01 / BEHIND THE PIPELINES

Engineering the platform.
Empowering the people.

I’m Nina Bee, a Lead Data Engineer focused on making data useful, dependable, and easier to work with.

My approach connects platform architecture with the people who depend on it. That means clear data models, intentional engineering practices, and a shared understanding of what good looks like.

I value simple solutions, thoughtful documentation, and teams that ask good questions. The goal is a data foundation that supports today’s decisions and tomorrow’s ideas.
